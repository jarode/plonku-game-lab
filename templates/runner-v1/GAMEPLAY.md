# Gameplay (runner-v1 seed)

Placeholder prototype. Art is the MIT example pack; change skins per game.

## Loop

1. **START** — Game scene, `GameStatus = Preparing`. Tap the runner or release a key / Space to start.
2. **PLAYING** — world scrolls left. Space or tap jumps. Score increases with time.
3. **GAME OVER** — hit a hazard.
4. **RETRY** — tap, Space, or R: in-place reset. No page reload.

Viewport: **540×960**, portrait.

Object names stay `Dino` / `CactusObstacle` / `IslandObstacle` / `WreckObstacle` even when art is something else.
